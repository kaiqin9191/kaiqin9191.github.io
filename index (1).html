Introduction

I am a PhD candidate in Management Science at the University of North Texas, in the Department of Information Technology and Decision Sciences (ITDS), G. Brint Ryan College of Business. My research examines how firms' operational and employment decisions shape workplace outcomes, with current projects on employee benefits and workplace safety, information transparency in supply chains, and organizational applications of artificial intelligence.
