Introduction

I am a PhD candidate in Management Science at the University of North Texas, in the Department of Information Technology and Decision Sciences (ITDS), G. Brint Ryan College of Business, advised by Dr. Victor Prybutok. My research asks which operating decisions actually change outcomes for workers, and which only mark the firms that were going to do well anyway.

My dissertation links firms' federal employee-benefit filings to their injury records and inspection histories, building a panel of 26,757 U.S. employers, and uses designs that separate differences between firms from changes within them. I also study how delivery information shapes customers' judgments of service, and how people engage with generative AI. I am currently instructor of record for the department's senior analytics capstone.
